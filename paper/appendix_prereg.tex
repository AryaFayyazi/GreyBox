\subsection*{Multiple tests}
The family is every registered endpoint, passed or failed, of every closed-agent test whose result the abstract cites, and of the tests that bear on those claims (37 endpoints; open-agent endpoints are left out). For each, a one-sided $p$-value against its registered bar comes from its estimate and its 95\% cluster-bootstrap interval (normal approximation, SE = interval width / 3.92; for an equivalence endpoint, the larger of its two one-sided $p$-values). Holm's step-down then keeps the family-wise error at 0.05. This check is exploratory: we chose it after the outcomes, and revised it after review (a first version mixed in two open-agent endpoints). Of the 37 endpoints, 26 passed as registered and 25 survive Holm; the one that passed but does not survive is GPT-6-luna's reviewer-free gate over random holds.
