\subsection*{Multiple tests}
The 15 preregistered endpoints behind the abstract's closed-agent claims form one family. For each, a one-sided $p$-value against its registered null comes from its estimate and its 95\% cluster-bootstrap interval (normal approximation, SE = interval width / 3.92); Holm's step-down then keeps the family-wise error at 0.05 (exploratory). All 15 survive. The weakest, GPT-6-luna's reviewer-free gate against random holds, would not survive a plain Bonferroni cut ($0.05/15$).
