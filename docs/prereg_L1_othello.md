# Preregistration L1: black-box recovery of Othello-GPT's hidden state (fresh games)

Written 2026-10-15 ~14:05 PDT, before any confirmation game was generated.

## Claim
Black-box queries alone (61 queries per position: the next-move distribution, plus the next-move distribution after
each of the 60 single-move suffixes) recover Othello-GPT's layer-6 hidden state almost completely. They recover more
than the input sequence does through the same decoder class.

## Frozen objects (trained only on the development games, seed 0; hashed below)
- `models/othello/demo.pt`:
  - the grey-box decoders θ(Ψ) → top-64 PCs of the true L6 residual (development PCA frame), and θ(Ψ) → board;
  - the white-box board probe on L6.
- `models/othello/input_only.pt`: the same MLP recipe on input-only features (which squares each side has placed).
- `scripts/19_othello_confirm.py` (stage=confirm).

## Data
1,000 fresh random legal games (seed 2026, never used), 10 positions per game, generated by the confirm stage after
this lock.

## Endpoints (game-cluster bootstrap, 2,000 resamples)
- **E1:** variance-weighted R² of the grey-box decoder on the top-64 development PCs of the true L6 state. Passes if
  the one-sided 95% lower bound exceeds 0.90.
- **E2:** R²(grey box) − R²(input-only) > 0. One-sided bootstrap p.
- **E3:** occupied-stone accuracy (mine vs theirs), grey box − input-only > 0. One-sided bootstrap p.
- E2 and E3 are Holm-corrected at α = 0.05.
- **Reported, not tested:** white-box probe occupied-stone accuracy (the ceiling reference).

## Reporting
All results are reported whatever their direction. Deviations are dated in this file.
